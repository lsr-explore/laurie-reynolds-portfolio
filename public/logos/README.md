# Portfolio logos

Local copies of organization and technology logos. See `sources.json` for each asset's source URL. Retrieved September 23, 2026.

Company logos come from official websites, except Walmart, Siemens, and Sony (Simple Icons 11) and Nodality (historical Crunchbase company logo). Code for San Jose uses the archived logo from its official repository, preserving the name in the portfolio. Inline SVG logos were extracted without changing their paths. The Walmart, Siemens, and Sony viewBoxes remove excess vertical whitespace; their paths are unchanged. Kofile uses a white logo and needs a dark background.

Technology icons are from Simple Icons 11 (https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.0.0), distributed under CC0-1.0. Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners. The ESLint icon identifies eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y; Testing Library identifies React Testing Library. Concepts and standards without a product logo remain text-only; no conformance or certification marks are used.

Walmart Global Tech uses the official white-and-green `LogoEXT_White.svg` from tech.walmart.com on a navy background. The earlier Walmart logo is retained as an unused asset.
